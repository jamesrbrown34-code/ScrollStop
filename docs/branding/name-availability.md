# Name availability: Stillpoint, Tidemark, Ebb, Nudge (and Ground, Drift, Hush)

Research date: 2026-08-09. Method: direct fetches of Google Play search/listings, DuckDuckGo HTML search, Bing, and the UK IPO / USPTO registries. Registry results below that could not be retrieved programmatically are flagged as **manual check required** — nothing in this file should be treated as a confirmed trademark clearance.

Play Store display names are **not unique** — Google allows duplicate app names — so "collision" here means brand/discoverability conflict and legal risk, not a hard technical blocker (the technical name that must be unique is the package ID, which is in your control).

## Stillpoint

**Play Store.** Search results are dominated by a game: "Stillpoint!: Minimal Defense" (Buff Studio Co., Ltd.) — a minimal roguelike tower-defense game, plus a paid "Stillpoint: Minimal Defense" SKU. No wellbeing app appeared on the first search page. However, a direct lookup of the listing found **"StillPoint: Mindfulness Inspo!"** by A1DAY1APPS (developer Devin Harris, US), category **Health & Fitness**, updated Feb 14 2025 — a mindfulness/meditation/journaling app. This is an exact-name match in your target category and is the real blocker. Note: it is a small app (10+ downloads) and did not surface on the first search-results page, but it exists and is indexed.

**Web / App Store (brand confusion).** "Stillpoint" is a well-populated wellness name:
- "Stillpoint Meditation" (App Store, id1419594731) — meditation app.
- "Stillpoint Meditation Tracker" (App Store) — meditation timer/journal.
- "Stillpoint — Quiet the World" (stillpoint.tools) — daily stillness/meditation web tool.
- "Stillpoint: Your Sanctuary for Mind & Focus" (stillpoint.vibratomstudios.com) — mindfulness/manifestation app.
- stillpointapp.ai — AI mental-health/wellness companion ("Sage"/"Atlas").
- stillpoint.day — short-form-video/screen-time awareness tool (closest in spirit to a doomscrolling nudge app).
- GitHub: SpartanGomez/stillpoint — offline meditation app.

**Trademark.** UK IPO and USPTO could not be queried programmatically (see Sources & limits). Given the number of live wellness products using the name, a registered mark in classes 9/42 (software) and/or 44 (health services) in the UK, US or EU is plausible but **unverified**. Manual registry check required.

**Verdict: Taken.** Exact-name wellbeing app already on Play plus dense meditation-brand usage off-store.

## Tidemark

**Play Store.** No wellbeing app. Top results: "Tidemark: Pair Match" (Jackie Scott Consultant — a tile/pair-matching game), "Tide Mark" (DA ACADEMY LIMITED — appears to be a harbour/tide log), plus a long tail of watermark apps (iWatermark, Timemark, etc.). Web search added "Tidemark FCU Mobile" — the banking app of Tidemark Federal Credit Union (US credit union). "Tide Mark" as two words and the credit union's "Tidemark" both trade on the name, but neither is a wellbeing/mindfulness app.

**Web / brand.** "Tidemark" is the name of a known US financial-planning software company (Tidemark, by AppFolio) and of Tidemark Federal Credit Union; also "Tidemark Creative LLC" (tidemark.app). The word is also generic in marine/tide contexts (a high-water mark).

**Trademark.** Registry not checked (manual required). Expect marks in financial/business software classes (36/42) for the finance brand; wellbeing/software marks unverified.

**Verdict: Available-ish** for a wellbeing app on Play, with the caveat that the Tidemark finance brand is established and you should still clear UK/US registers before launch.

### Manual checks still required (human)
1. **UK IPO** — run a "Search for a trade mark" at `https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk/ipo-tmtext` for each name (word-only, UK). ~5 minutes, free. Focus on classes 9 (software/apps), 42 (SaaS), 44 (health), 41 (education/mindfulness), 45 (wellbeing services). Look for live (not dead/expired) marks.
2. **USPTO** — run `https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/search-information` (Basic Word Mark search) for each name, same classes, if US expansion is planned.
3. **EUIPO (optional)** — `https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/` if EU launch matters (this was outside scope; not attempted).
4. **Play Store final sanity check** — search Play directly in a browser for the chosen name at launch time; store results change and the stillpoint "Mindfulness Inspo!" listing shows collision risk is live.

### Caveats
- Play Store search results are personalised and paginated; the exact-name "StillPoint: Mindfulness Inspo!" app did not appear on the first Stillpoint search page but was confirmed via its details URL. Re-verify at launch.
- No trademark database was successfully queried, so every "trademark risk" rating above is an inference from product/brand usage, **not** a registered-mark finding. The registry checks are a required next step, not a formality.
- Verdicts reflect the four/six sources above at the research date; app stores change quickly.
