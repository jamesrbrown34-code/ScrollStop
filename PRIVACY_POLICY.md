# ScrollBeat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

ScrollBeat ("we", "our", "the app") is an Android app that helps you notice and reduce mindless scrolling.

## What we collect

ScrollBeat **does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data**. There is no account, no sign-up, no login, and no server. Everything happens on your device.

- **Scroll counts**: When you enable the accessibility service and choose apps to track, ScrollBeat counts scroll events in those apps. These counts are stored **only on your device** and are used solely to show you your statistics and to decide when to show a reminder.
- **Settings**: Your preferences (tracked apps, reminder limit, tone, quiet hours, pause state, subscription status) are stored **only on your device**.
- **No browsing, reading, or recording**: ScrollBeat does not read screen contents, capture text, log in to anything, or record what you see.

## Accessibility service

ScrollBeat uses an Android accessibility service **only** to receive scroll-event notifications from the apps you choose to track. It does not retrieve window content, does not read text, and does not interact with other apps. You must explicitly enable the service and choose which apps to monitor; you can disable or remove it at any time.

## Notifications

ScrollBeat asks for notification permission so it can show scroll reminders. Notifications are generated on your device.

## Subscriptions

Premium is an optional subscription (monthly or yearly) with a free trial. Payment and purchase management are handled entirely by **Google Play** (the Google Play billing service), not by ScrollBeat. ScrollBeat only reads whether you have an active subscription to unlock features. For how Google handles your data, see Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## Permissions we use

- Accessibility service: to count scroll events (optional, user-enabled).
- Notifications: to show scroll reminders (optional, user-enabled).
- Vibrate: to accompany reminders.
- Full-screen intent: to show a full-screen reminder where the platform allows it.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect any personal information from anyone.
