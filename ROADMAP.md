# Roadmap

## Current Phase

* Build reliable scroll session detection.
* Provide clear interventions at escalating risk thresholds.
* Show local daily progress so users can understand today's behavior.
* Detect and display scroll pace for high-intensity sessions.
* Keep daily analytics accurate with first-scroll session starts and idle session reset.
* Keep the dashboard accessible on smaller screens as metrics expand.
* Let users temporarily snooze interventions without disabling tracking.

## Next Phase

* Add weekly reports with personal bests and trend insights.
* Add app-specific limits and snooze controls.

## Future Phase

* Add adaptive intervention timing based on effectiveness.
* Add mood check-ins and intervention feedback loops.
* Add privacy-preserving accountability partner summaries.

## Long-Term Vision

* Predict doomscrolling risk before users lose agency.
* Support social challenges and leaderboards without shame mechanics.
* Monetize advanced controls and analytics while keeping core behavior-change tools free.
